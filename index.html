Cleanroom Gowning Procedure

Step-by-step gowning sequence for personnel entering a sterile manufacturing area

ISO 14644 · EU GMP Annex 1 · Grade B / C
Note: Always gown in the sequence shown, moving from the “dirty” to the “clean” side of the gowning room. Never touch the outer surface of sterile garments or gloves with bare hands. Any breach of gowning integrity must be corrected before entering the critical area.
